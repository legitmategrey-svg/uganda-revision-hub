Capital letters and full stops

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P3 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Use capital letters at the beginning of sentences
  • Use capital letters for names
  • End statements with a full stop

Lesson notes

Begin a sentence with a capital letter. Use capital letters for names of people and places, days of the week, and the word I.

A full stop (.) comes at the end of a statement. It tells the reader that the sentence has ended.

Read your sentence again to check that it begins with a capital letter and ends with the correct punctuation.

Worked example

incorrect: mary lives in kampala correct: Mary lives in Kampala.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving capital letters and full stops. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Rewrite correctly: john goes to school.
  2. Add the missing full stop: We play netball
  3. Write one sentence about your school.
Show suggested answers
  1. John goes to school.
  2. We play netball.
  3. Answers will vary; check capital letter and full stop.

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
