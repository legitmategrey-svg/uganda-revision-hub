Fractions, decimals and percentages

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P7 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Convert common fractions to decimals and percentages
  • Compare quantities in different forms
  • Solve practical percentage problems

Lesson notes

Fractions, decimals and percentages are different ways of describing parts of a whole. To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. To convert a decimal to a percentage, multiply by 100 and add the % sign.

Common equivalents include 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%, 1/4 = 0.25 = 25%, and 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.

In percentage problems, identify the whole quantity first. If a question asks for 15% of 200, calculate 15/100 × 200.

Worked example

A bag costs UGX 40,000 and has a 10% discount. Discount = 10/100 × 40,000 = UGX 4,000. Sale price = UGX 36,000.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving fractions, decimals and percentages. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Convert 3/5 to a decimal and percentage.
  2. Find 15% of 240.
  3. A learner scores 42 out of 60. Express the score as a percentage.
Show suggested answers
  1. 0.6 and 60%
  2. 36
  3. 42/60 × 100 = 70%

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
