S6 • Biology

Genetics: genes, alleles and inheritance

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download S6 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Distinguish genes and alleles
  • Explain genotype and phenotype
  • Use a simple genetic cross to predict outcomes

Lesson notes

A gene is a section of DNA associated with a characteristic or biological function. Alleles are different forms of the same gene. In diploid organisms, an individual generally has two alleles for each autosomal gene, one inherited from each parent.

The genotype is the allele combination an organism carries, such as TT, Tt or tt. The phenotype is the observable characteristic produced by the genotype and the environment.

In a simple dominant-recessive model, a dominant allele can mask the effect of a recessive allele in a heterozygote. Real inheritance can be more complex, and not all traits follow this pattern.

Worked example

For T = tall (dominant) and t = short (recessive), Tt × Tt can produce TT, Tt, Tt and tt. The expected genotype ratio is 1:2:1 and, under this simple model, the phenotype ratio is 3 tall : 1 short.

Scenario-based revision task

In pea plants, tallness (T) is dominant to shortness (t). Two heterozygous tall plants are crossed: Tt × Tt. Use a genetic cross to predict possible offspring.

Apply your learning

  1. Explain the difference between genotype and phenotype. (2 marks)
    Suggested answer: Genotype is the allele combination, e.g. Tt; phenotype is the observable characteristic, e.g. tall.
  2. Complete a Punnett square or list the possible offspring genotypes. (2 marks)
    Suggested answer: TT, Tt, Tt, tt.
  3. Find the expected fraction of short offspring and explain. (2 marks)
    Suggested answer: 1/4 (25%) are tt; only tt expresses the recessive short phenotype.
Teacher-style marking guide

Award marks for accurate terms, correct gametes/cross, offspring combinations, and interpreting the 1:2:1 genotype ratio / 3:1 phenotype ratio.

Practice questions

  1. What is an allele?
  2. State the difference between genotype and phenotype.
  3. For Tt × Tt, what fraction of offspring are expected to be tt?
Show suggested answers
  1. An alternative form of a gene
  2. Genotype is the allele combination; phenotype is the observable characteristic
  3. 1/4 or 25%

How to present answers

  1. Read: underline the command word (state, describe, explain, calculate, compare or suggest).
  2. Use evidence: select facts or figures from the question scenario.
  3. Explain: give a clear reason or show each calculation step, including units where needed.
  4. Conclude: state the final answer or recommendation directly.

Marking guide: award credit for relevant facts, correct method, application to the scenario, and a justified conclusion. Accept other scientifically/mathematically correct answers.
