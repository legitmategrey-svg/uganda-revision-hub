S4 • Mathematics

Statistics: mean, median, mode and range

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download S4 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Calculate the mean of a data set
  • Find the median and mode
  • Calculate the range and interpret results

Lesson notes

The mean is the sum of all observations divided by the number of observations. The median is the middle value after arranging the data in order. The mode is the value that occurs most often.

The range is the largest value minus the smallest value. It gives a simple measure of spread.

For an even number of observations, the median is the mean of the two middle values after arranging the data.

Worked example

For 2, 3, 3, 6, 11: mean = 25/5 = 5; median = 3; mode = 3; range = 11 - 2 = 9.

Scenario-based revision task

A health club records the number of minutes five learners spent walking: 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12. The teacher wants one measure of typical activity and a measure of spread.

Apply your learning

  1. Calculate the mean walking time. (2 marks)
    Suggested answer: (4 + 6 + 8 + 10 + 12) ÷ 5 = 40 ÷ 5 = 8 minutes.
  2. State the median. (1 mark)
    Suggested answer: 8 minutes.
  3. Calculate the range and explain what it tells the teacher. (2 marks)
    Suggested answer: 12 − 4 = 8 minutes; the longest and shortest times differ by 8 minutes.
Teacher-style marking guide
