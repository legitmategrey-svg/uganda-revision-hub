Naming words (nouns)

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P1 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain what a naming word is
  • Identify people, animals, places and things
  • Use naming words in simple sentences

Lesson notes

A naming word is called a noun. It names a person, animal, place or thing.

Examples of people are teacher, mother and child. Examples of animals are goat, cow and bird. Examples of places are school, home and market. Examples of things are book, cup and ball.

A sentence can contain a naming word: The girl has a book. The words girl and book are nouns.

Worked example

In “The cow eats grass,” cow and grass are naming words.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving naming words (nouns). Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Underline the nouns: The boy kicks a ball.
  2. Name two animals and two things found in a classroom.
  3. Make one sentence using the word teacher.
Show suggested answers
  1. boy, ball
  2. Possible answers: goat, hen; book, desk
  3. Answers will vary; e.g. The teacher reads a book.

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
