The human respiratory system

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P7 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Name the main parts of the respiratory system
  • Explain inhalation and exhalation
  • Describe how to keep the respiratory system healthy

Lesson notes

The respiratory system takes oxygen into the body and removes carbon dioxide. Main parts include the nose, trachea, bronchi, lungs and diaphragm.

During inhalation, the diaphragm contracts and flattens, the chest cavity becomes larger, and air enters the lungs. During exhalation, the diaphragm relaxes, the chest cavity becomes smaller, and air moves out.

In the lungs, oxygen passes from air sacs into the blood while carbon dioxide passes from the blood into the air sacs to be breathed out.

Worked example

During running, breathing becomes faster because muscles need more oxygen and produce more carbon dioxide.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving the human respiratory system. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Name the muscle that helps change the size of the chest cavity.
  2. Where does gas exchange occur in the lungs?
  3. Give two ways of keeping the respiratory system healthy.
Show suggested answers
  1. Diaphragm
  2. Air sacs (alveoli)
  3. Examples: avoid smoking and smoke; exercise; reduce dust exposure; seek treatment for respiratory infections

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
