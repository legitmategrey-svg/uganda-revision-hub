S2 • Biology

Nutrition in plants

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download S2 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Define photosynthesis
  • State the raw materials and conditions for photosynthesis
  • Write the word equation for photosynthesis

Lesson notes

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy absorbed by chlorophyll to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen.

Carbon dioxide enters leaves mainly through stomata. Water is absorbed by roots and transported through xylem. Chlorophyll in chloroplasts absorbs light energy.

Photosynthesis needs light, chlorophyll, carbon dioxide and water. Glucose may be used in respiration, stored as starch, or used to make other substances.

Worked example

Carbon dioxide + water —(light and chlorophyll)→ glucose + oxygen.

Scenario-based revision task

A farmer notices that bean plants kept in a dark store are pale and grow poorly, while similar plants outside are green. Both receive water.

Apply your learning

  1. Name the green pigment involved in photosynthesis. (1 mark)
    Suggested answer: Chlorophyll.
  2. Explain why the plants in the dark become pale and grow poorly. (2 marks)
    Suggested answer: They receive insufficient light for photosynthesis; they make less food and chlorophyll development is affected.
  3. Suggest two conditions the farmer should provide for healthy growth and explain why. (3 marks)
    Suggested answer: Adequate light and water; carbon dioxide and mineral nutrients are also needed. Light supplies energy for photosynthesis; water and carbon dioxide are raw materials.
Teacher-style marking guide

Award marks for naming chlorophyll, linking light to photosynthesis/food production, and giving relevant conditions with reasons.

Practice questions

  1. Name the green pigment needed for photosynthesis.
  2. Where do plants obtain carbon dioxide?
  3. Why is light needed for photosynthesis?
Show suggested answers
  1. Chlorophyll
  2. From the air, entering mainly through stomata
  3. It provides energy for the process.

How to present answers

  1. Read: underline the command word (state, describe, explain, calculate, compare or suggest).
  2. Use evidence: select facts or figures from the question scenario.
  3. Explain: give a clear reason or show each calculation step, including units where needed.
  4. Conclude: state the final answer or recommendation directly.

Marking guide: award credit for relevant facts, correct method, application to the scenario, and a justified conclusion. Accept other scientifically/mathematically correct answers.
