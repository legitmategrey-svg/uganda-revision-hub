Percentages

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P6 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain percent as parts out of 100
  • Convert simple fractions to percentages
  • Find a percentage of a quantity

Lesson notes

Percent means “out of one hundred” and is written with the % sign. For example, 25% means 25 out of 100, or 25/100.

To find a percentage of a quantity, change the percentage to a fraction over 100 and multiply by the quantity, or find an easier fraction first.

10% is one-tenth; 50% is one-half; 25% is one-quarter.

Worked example

25% of 80 = 25/100 × 80 = 20.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving percentages. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Write 3/4 as a percentage.
  2. Find 10% of 350.
  3. A school has 200 pupils. If 40% are girls, how many girls are there?
Show suggested answers
  1. 75%
  2. 35
  3. 80 girls

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
