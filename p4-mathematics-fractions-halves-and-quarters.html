Fractions: halves and quarters

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P4 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain numerator and denominator
  • Recognise one-half and one-quarter
  • Find simple fractions of shapes or objects

Lesson notes

A fraction shows equal parts of a whole. The denominator is the bottom number and tells how many equal parts make the whole. The numerator is the top number and tells how many parts are being considered.

One-half is written 1/2. One-quarter is written 1/4. The parts must be equal for the fraction to be fair.

To find one-half of 8 objects, share them into 2 equal groups: 8 ÷ 2 = 4. To find one-quarter of 8, share into 4 equal groups: 8 ÷ 4 = 2.

Worked example

One-quarter of 12 = 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving fractions: halves and quarters. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. In 3/5, identify the numerator and denominator.
  2. Find one-half of 18.
  3. Find one-quarter of 20.
Show suggested answers
  1. Numerator 3; denominator 5
  2. 9
  3. 5

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
