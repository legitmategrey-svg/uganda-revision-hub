Action words (verbs)

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P2 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Identify verbs as action words
  • Choose verbs that fit a sentence
  • Write simple sentences with verbs

Lesson notes

A verb is a word that tells what someone or something does. Examples include run, jump, read, cook, sing and write.

A sentence usually needs a verb to tell what is happening. We say “The baby sleeps,” not “The baby sleep” when talking about one baby in the present simple.

Some verbs describe actions we cannot easily see, such as think, know and like.

Worked example

“The pupils sing a song.” The verb is sing.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving action words (verbs). Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Find the verb: The dog barks loudly.
  2. Complete: A bird ___ in the sky. (flies / table)
  3. Write a sentence using the verb read.
Show suggested answers
  1. barks
  2. flies
  3. Answers will vary.

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
