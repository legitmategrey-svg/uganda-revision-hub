Addition of two-digit numbers

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P2 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain addition as putting groups together
  • Add two-digit numbers without carrying
  • Check an addition answer

Lesson notes

Addition means joining groups to find the total. The plus sign is + and the equals sign is =.

Line up tens under tens and ones under ones. Add the ones first, then add the tens.

You can check by counting forward or by reversing the operation using subtraction when you know it.

Worked example

23 + 14: ones 3 + 4 = 7; tens 2 tens + 1 ten = 3 tens. Therefore 23 + 14 = 37.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving addition of two-digit numbers. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. 12 + 15 = ?
  2. 34 + 22 = ?
  3. A pupil has 21 pencils and gets 13 more. How many pencils are there altogether?
Show suggested answers
  1. 27
  2. 56
  3. 34 pencils

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
