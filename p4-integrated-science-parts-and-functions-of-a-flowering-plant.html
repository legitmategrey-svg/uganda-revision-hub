Parts and functions of a flowering plant

Lesson notes • worked example • practice • suggested answers

Download P4 notes PDF

Learning support material. Follow your current school syllabus and teacher’s guidance.

Learning outcomes

  • Name common parts of a flowering plant
  • Describe the function of roots, stem, leaves and flowers
  • Explain why plants need water and sunlight

Lesson notes

The main parts of a flowering plant include roots, stem, leaves and flowers. Some plants also produce fruits and seeds.

Roots anchor the plant and absorb water and mineral salts from the soil. The stem supports the plant and carries water and food. Leaves make food using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide. Flowers help many plants reproduce by forming seeds after pollination and fertilisation.

Plants need water, light, air and suitable nutrients to grow well.

Worked example

A bean plant with damaged roots may wilt because it cannot absorb enough water.

Everyday-life activity

At home or at school, a learner meets a situation involving parts and functions of a flowering plant. Use the lesson idea to answer the practice questions and explain how you know your answer is correct.

Practice questions

  1. Give two functions of roots.
  2. Which plant part makes food?
  3. Name two things plants need to grow.
Show suggested answers
  1. Anchor the plant; absorb water and mineral salts
  2. Leaves
  3. Any two: water, light, air, nutrients

How to present answers

  1. Write neatly and answer what the question asks.
  2. For calculations, show the number sentence and answer.
  3. For explanations, give one clear fact or reason.

Suggested marking: 1 mark for each correct fact/step unless the teacher's marking guide says otherwise.

Revision checklist

  • I can explain the main idea in my own words.
  • I can solve a new question without copying the example.
  • I can check my answer and correct mistakes.
