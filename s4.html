Candidate study checklist

  • Revise every topic in your current syllabus.
  • Practise with a timer and exam-style instructions.
  • Use answers and marking guides to identify gaps.
  • Keep a notebook of mistakes and revisit them weekly.
  • Confirm paper year, subject and source permissions before sharing or printing.
