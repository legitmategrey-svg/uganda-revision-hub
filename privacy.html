Privacy Policy

This site may use Firebase Authentication to manage learner accounts. Study notes you type into the personal-notes field are stored in the current browser on the current device unless a feature explicitly says otherwise. Do not enter passwords or sensitive personal information into study notes. If the site is hosted in a public repository, files published as static pages, PDFs or JSON may be accessible to anyone.
